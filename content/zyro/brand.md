- Core idea / tagline: **"Nothing Unnecessary"**

- Community section: "ZYRO COMMUNITY" (social / UGC block)

## Campaign art direction (from lifestyle banners and listing images)

- Headlines in heavy condensed caps: "FULL ON FUN, ZYRO GUILT", "EVERYDAY HYDRATION, ZERO WORRIES.", "ZERO SUGAR ZERO CALORIES / NOTHING UNNECESSARY"
- A giant high-contrast serif "0" is a recurring motif behind the product
- Every product's listing banner uses that product's colour as a full-bleed background
- Can label system: solid colour body, vertical script "zyro" logo with a swash line running to the top, a product-line badge (lightning = Energy Pop, paisley = Indie Pop, bubbles = Skinny Pop, a person drinking = Hydrate+), condensed caps "ZERO SUGAR / <FLAVOUR> / ZERO CALORIES / 250ml"
- Hydrate+ is a 400 ml PET bottle; every other line is a 250 ml slim can

## Story themes worth carrying into the rebuild

- **Subtraction / minimalism**: "what you remove matters more than what you add" (from both music and drinks)
- **Roots**: "India is home to me", desi nostalgia (Indie Pop: Masala Cola, Jeera Soda)
- **Guilt-free taste**: zero sugar and zero calories, without giving up flavour
- **Clarity, discipline, purpose**: values the brand claims
- **Music / culture**: founder is an artist; concert ticket contests on his tour (see campaigns.md)
